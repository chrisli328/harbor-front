Art
  3D world (round 2): ships, harbor, islands, sea and fog are built in code from three.js
  primitives (js/look.js, js/models.js, js/terrain.js); no model files.
  Portraits, hero card, icons and UI sprites are AI-generated for this prototype
  (Codex built-in image generation), then cut and keyed with tools/cut.py and tools/patches.py.
  No third-party game art is shipped. Top Force: Commander screenshots were used only on a local
  side-by-side comparison page that is not part of this public copy.
